# Privacy Policy for Tappy

**Effective Date:** November 4, 2026  
**Application Name:** Tappy (`com.tappy.sensorygame`)

## 1. Overview & Commitment to Children's Privacy
**Tappy** is a sensory touch game designed for babies, infants, and toddlers, with controls for parents. We are committed to protecting the privacy of children and families and complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K), and Google Play's **Designed for Families** policy.

## 2. Zero Data Collection
**Tappy does not collect, store, use, or share any personal or non-personal information from any user (adult or child).**
- **No Internet Access:** The application does not request the `android.permission.INTERNET` permission and operates 100% offline on your device.
- **No Accounts or Registration:** The app never asks for names, email addresses, ages, photos, locations, or device identifiers.
- **No Analytics or Crash Trackers:** The app contains zero third-party analytics, tracking, or telemetry SDKs.
- **No Advertisements or In-App Purchases:** The app contains no advertising networks and no in-app purchases or external links.

## 3. Device Permissions Used
Tappy requests only two standard hardware capabilities required for local gameplay:
- **`android.permission.VIBRATE`:** Used solely to provide gentle tactile vibration feedback when shapes or musical keys are tapped (can be turned off at any time in the Parent Hub).
- **`android.permission.WAKE_LOCK`:** Used solely to keep the screen from dimming while the app is actively open on screen.

## 4. Screen Pinning & Parent Gate
Tappy uses Android's built-in Screen Pinning API (`StartLockTask` / `StopLockTask`) so parents can optionally pin the app on screen during baby play. A parent verification gate in the top-right corner allows adults to unpin and exit the application at any time.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the support email listed on the Google Play Store listing page for **Tappy**.
